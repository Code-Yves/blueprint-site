About

Hi, I’m Yves Eggleston

Whether it’s a custom home, a multi-unit building, or a commercial space, I can help you take your project from the design and permit stages all the way to occupancy.

My journey in construction began in 2005. I only take on a limited number of projects at a time and by staying small, I can focus on your project. I’m on-site in person to keep an eye on every detail, ensuring that the work happening behind the walls is just as solid as the work you see on the outside.

I work in the open. I share receipts, discounts, and quotes with you directly. Costs are billed at cost, with no hidden markups.

I’ve lived in Ottawa my whole life. I’m a proud husband and a father of two, and I treat every project with the same care I’d put into my own home.

A reputation built on staying put

Twenty years of consistency.

I have been building in Ottawa since 2005. I finish what I start. When an issue comes up, I work to resolve it directly with owners. I have had the same email address for over 20 years.

Not every project goes exactly to plan. I stay with the work. Most of my work has come by word of mouth. No lawn signs, no social media. My work speaks for itself.

How I work

An adaptable process. Step in for a roadblock, or manage the whole job.

  1. Feasibility

    Site, numbers, and what you actually want, before drawings that don’t fit the budget.

  2. Design & permits

    I steer drawings, engineering, and permits, and I flag what the paper plans miss.

  3. A working budget

    Trade quotes near the end of design, then a final budget.

  4. Build & timeline

    Day-to-day site run: trades, inspections, suppliers, a timeline you can plan around.

  5. After-care

    Small issues show up after handover. I stay available.

  6. Financing

    I have worked with private lenders on past Blueprint jobs.
