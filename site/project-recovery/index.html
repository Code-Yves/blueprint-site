How I can help

A mediator when things break down

I sit down with you and the trade, look at the agreement and the work, and find a fix that gets the job moving again. Where there is a warranty, I help with that work too.

A clear plan to get unstuck

I review the project, what went wrong and what it will honestly take to finish. The first look is on me. I agree the scope in writing before any paid work, and I can stay on as your person on site.

Money to finish the job

I show you what finishing still costs, so you can talk to a lender or investor with real numbers.

Someone in your corner

Your project stays yours. If a takeover or buyout comes up, I help you read the offers and push for fair terms.

For banks and other lenders

Do you hold a stalled construction loan? I review the work in place and the cost still left, so you can decide your next step. This review is not an appraisal, a regulated certification or legal advice.

What I do not do

I am not a lawyer, an appraiser or a licensed adjuster, and I do not give legal advice or legal opinions. I cannot promise funding, approvals, dates, warranty repairs, claim payments, a fair outcome or that everyone will agree. I will always tell you plainly what I can and cannot do.
