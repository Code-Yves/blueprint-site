Project Recovery

Your project, back on track.

I work with banks, investors, realtors, lawyers, and owners managing their own builds in Ottawa.

Whether a project has stalled or become more than you can manage, I help you understand where it stands and what comes next.

Who it is for

Banks, investors, realtors, lawyers, and owners.

These are people in Ottawa managing a build who need help completing it. A project may have stalled, or it may have become more than you can manage. That is not a verdict on you. I look at what is in front of me and help you find a way forward.

  • Disputes over incomplete or poor trade work
  • Permits that have stalled
  • Trades that are missing
  • Money running out
  • A lender stepping back
  • A builder who has left
  • Warranty repairs still outstanding

How I can help

A mediator when things break down

I help you and the trade look at the agreements, the work, and a resolution that can get the job moving again. Where a warranty exists, I can support that warranty work. I am not a court. I do not represent you as a lawyer. I cannot promise that everyone will agree.

A clear plan to get unstuck

I review the project, what went wrong, and what finishing honestly involves. The first look is on me. Before any paid work, I agree the scope in writing. If you want, I stay on as your person on site.

Money to finish the job

I can help you see what finishing still costs. I do not promise funding, and I do not promise that a lender will step in.

Someone in your corner

I do not take over your project. If a takeover or a buyout is on the table, I help you read the options and the proposals and seek fair terms. I cannot promise that the outcome will be fair.

More ways I can help

A written cost to complete

An honest written cost for the work that is still left.

Replacing a builder or trade

If someone has left, I can scope the remaining work, gather quotes, and help you choose who finishes. I cannot promise that the right trade will be free to start.

Permit status

I review the permit file with the City of Ottawa and name the steps that are still required. I do not promise approval, and I do not promise that a date will be met.

New home warranty

I help you identify and document concerns and prepare the submissions that fit your situation. What is covered, and the deadlines, depend on the home and the warranty. Not every concern is eligible, and a repair is not guaranteed.

Liens and holdbacks

I organize the paperwork, tell you when a lawyer should look at it, and help you connect with a construction lawyer. I do not give legal opinions. I do not interpret legal deadlines. I do not resolve title or lien rights.

Adjudication

I organize project records, payment documents, and evidence of the work done. I do not represent you, and I do not give legal advice.

Damage on site

After a fire, a storm, a theft, or water damage, I document the condition and estimate the repair so you can speak with the insurer. I am not a licensed adjuster, and I do not promise that a claim will be paid.

Power of sale

I assess the work still to do and share building and cost information with you and with the lawyer or advisers on the file. I do not promise when that information will be ready, and I do not advise on enforcement or the law.

Last steps to occupancy

I coordinate the remaining work, the inspections, and the signoffs that lead toward occupancy. I do not guarantee a permit, and I do not guarantee a move in date.

For banks and other lenders

Do you hold a stalled construction loan? I can review the work in place and the cost still left, so you can decide your next step. This review is not an appraisal, not a regulated certification, and not legal advice.

If a project has stalled or become more than you can manage, talk to me before the next move.
